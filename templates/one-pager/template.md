# Template: One-Pager Pitch (1 slide, all audiences)

## When to use
- You have 2 minutes or less
- The audience is very busy (C-suite, investor at a networking event)
- You need to leave something behind that speaks for itself
- First contact / cold pitch

## The one-pager formula

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HEADLINE: [Single sentence that captures the whole idea]   │
│  e.g. "We reduce hospital readmission rates by 40% using AI"│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  THE PROBLEM                  │  OUR SOLUTION                │
│  [1-2 sentences, quantified]  │  [1-2 sentences, specific]   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WHY NOW           │  PROOF               │  THE ASK          │
│  [1 reason the     │  [1 data point or    │  [1 specific       │
│  timing is right]  │  testimonial]        │  next step]        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Rules
- Headline is a complete sentence with a number or a verb
- Problem must be felt by the audience (not just described)
- Solution must be described in language the audience uses, not the builder's language
- "Why Now" answers: why this can't wait / why this moment is different
- Proof is the single most credible data point you have
- Ask is specific: "15 minutes next week", "an intro to your Head of Engineering", "a pilot agreement"

## Examples

**Bad headline:** "AI-powered healthcare platform"
**Good headline:** "We cut hospital readmissions by 40% — saving $800K per hospital per year"

**Bad ask:** "We'd love your support"
**Good ask:** "Can we schedule a 20-minute pilot discussion with your operations team?"

## Speaker version (2-minute verbal pitch)
```
"[Problem — 1 sentence they feel]
[What we do — 1 sentence, plain language]
[Proof — 1 number or story]
[Why now — 1 sentence]
[Ask — 1 specific next step]"
```
Total: ~120 words. Practice until it's under 90 seconds.
