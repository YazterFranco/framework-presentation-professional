## Step 3: Final slide content (humanized)

### Slide 1
**Our team shipped 3 major features in the first half of 2026**
- 4 engineers, 6 months, 0 production incidents
- Completed auth redesign, payments v2, and mobile API
- Team is healthy and delivery is consistent

*Speaker note: "I want to start with something we should both feel good about. This team has delivered. That's the baseline for what I'm about to ask."*

---

### Slide 2
**H2 scope has doubled — and we're running out of runway**
- Q4 roadmap adds 5 new workstreams vs. H1's 3
- Current team is at 110% capacity with no buffer for incidents
- At this pace, 2 of 5 workstreams will slip past Q4

*Speaker note: "Here's the problem. The business moved fast — which is a good sign. But the team can't absorb this scope without something giving. And what gives first is always the things no one sees until it's too late."*

---

### Slide 3
**2 mid-level hires by September keep us on track**
- Covers 2 of the 5 at-risk workstreams
- Onboarding in parallel with existing team — no ramp disruption
- Positions us to handle H1 2027 scope without another emergency ask

*Speaker note: "The recommendation is straightforward: 2 people. Not 5. Not a reorg. Just 2 people who can be productive by October if we start the process now."*

---

### Slide 4
**Each hire pays back 6x in unlocked revenue**
- Delayed workstreams are tied to the enterprise tier launch ($1.6M ARR opportunity)
- Each FTE costs ~$130K/year fully loaded
- Net: $800K per hire in opportunity cost if we don't act

*Speaker note: "I know headcount is always a hard sell. So I want to put a number to it. This isn't a comfort ask — it's a leverage decision."*

---

### Slide 5
**We need approval by August 15 to hit a September start**
- Recruiting takes 6-8 weeks minimum
- August 15 approval → September 30 start → Q4 contribution
- Delay past August 15 pushes first contribution to Q1 2027

*Speaker note: "Here's the ask: one decision, today or this week. Approve 2 FTEs. The window is narrow — if we miss August 15, we've effectively decided to slip Q4."*
